# Privacy incident

1. Stop the affected intake/output path and revoke the narrowest relevant token or key.
2. Preserve hashes, audit events, configuration versions, and infrastructure logs; do not copy raw media/contact data into tickets or chat.
3. Determine whether raw media escaped the in-memory boundary, safe media became public, retention failed, or logs/traces contain prohibited content.
4. Invoke the legal/DPO notification process and evidence deadlines. Engineering does not decide reportability alone.
5. Delete exposed copies only after evidence preservation is authorized; rotate keys and invalidate sessions as required.
6. Prove containment with fingerprint searches across storage, queues, dead letters, logs, traces, exports, and backups.
7. Keep public activation closed until the DPO records remediation approval and a repeat privacy drill passes.
