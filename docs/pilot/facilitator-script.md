# Controlled pilot facilitator script

Status: draft requiring FRSC, legal/privacy, and clinical approval.

1. Confirm the environment banner says `CONTROLLED TEST` and public WhatsApp activation is off.
2. Present the approved notice in the participant's preferred supported language; record only the
   approved evidence reference in the protected participant register.
3. State: this exercise must not delay or replace a real call to FRSC 122/112. Stop immediately for
   a real emergency and follow FRSC direction.
4. Rehearse a safe voice-first, photo-first, and missing-location flow with non-sensitive fixtures.
   Nobody enters traffic, photographs a real victim, or shares a real phone/token in test evidence.
5. Ask the participant to explain when to share a pin, what confirmation means, how approved guidance
   differs from dispatch, and how to ask for help or withdraw.
6. Record pass/rehearsal-needed against a pseudonymous participant reference. Do not coach a failed
   answer into a pass without repeating the exercise.
7. Record system/provider failures, confusing wording, accessibility barriers, and stop decisions.
8. End by repeating the live emergency fallback and confirming whether the participant wishes to
   receive optional recognition after a dispatcher-verified contribution.
