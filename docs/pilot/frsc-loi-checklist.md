# FRSC letter-of-intent checklist

The signed letter is externally supplied; this checklist prevents an ambiguous letter being treated
as pilot authority.

- Named FRSC authority, command, corridor and validity period
- Controlled/staged versus live activity explicitly authorized
- First10 framed as decision support complementary to 122/112 and the FRSC app
- Named dispatcher/operations contact and agreed hours/escalation/stop authority
- Queue priority, verification, singleton timer, statuses and crew-briefing handoff acknowledged
- Baseline/evaluation access and permitted publication agreed
- Privacy roles, incident reporting, retention and processor review referenced
- No production SLA, medical practice, payment, NYSC credit, or unapproved public endorsement implied
- Quote/name/logo/publication permissions explicit
- Signatures, dates, evidence custodian and review/termination mechanism present
