# Second-corridor expression-of-interest checklist

- Written by an authorized FRSC command or partner agency
- Names the prospective corridor and operational problem
- States interest in a post-pilot discovery/replication discussion, not a production commitment
- Acknowledges that local workflow, gazetteer, languages, DPIA, clinical content and approvals must be
  revalidated
- Contains no unsupported outcome, SLA, funding, exclusivity, patient-data, or endorsement claim
- Includes signatory, date, contact channel, evidence custodian and publication permission
