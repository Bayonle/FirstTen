# Ten-slide defence deck outline

Every quantitative statement links to the approved evaluation evidence and identifies its cohort.

1. The corridor response-delay problem and measured baseline
2. Why bystander WhatsApp complements FRSC 122/112 and the existing app
3. The reporter journey: safe intake, vernacular guidance, truthful status
4. Privacy architecture: in-memory raw image, blurred derivative, minimized AI boundary
5. Dispatcher journey: manual fallback, corroboration, conflicts, timeline and briefing handoff
6. Technical architecture: one API, one worker, modular monolith, PostgreSQL/Wolverine, Aspire local
7. Pilot design: partners, cohort, consent/notice, baseline, staged/live distinction
8. Results: latency, completeness, false positives, guidance, closure and limitations
9. Safety, governance and failure evidence: clinical approval, DPIA, recovery, retention, stop gates
10. Replication and ask: second-corridor interest, what changes locally, next controlled decision

Appendix material may answer predictable questions about FRSC channels, AI error, provider outage,
clinical boundaries, NDPA, and why First10 does not revive an unapproved first-aid/NYSC programme.
