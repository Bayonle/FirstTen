# Reporter onboarding content specification

This is an approval-ready specification, not approved legal or clinical copy. FRSC, legal/privacy,
and clinical owners must approve the exact English, Pidgin, and Yoruba text/audio assets before use.

The onboarding and first-contact experience must:

1. Label Telegram and sandbox WhatsApp as controlled testing where applicable.
2. State that First10 complements—not replaces—FRSC 122/112 and that immediate danger should be
   reported through the established emergency channel.
3. Explain what to send (voice/photo/location), that the reporter must stay safe, and never ask them
   to approach traffic, victims, fire, or other hazards.
4. Explain server-side face blurring, approved processor use, access, retention, contact purpose,
   withdrawal/help, and the legally approved lawful basis or consent flow in plain language.
5. Explain that AI supports triage; dispatchers make operational decisions; guidance is limited to
   clinically approved templates.
6. Explain recognition as optional, private by default, withdrawable, non-monetary, and unrelated to
   emergency service priority or NYSC credit.
7. Provide an accessible help/withdrawal route and the version/date of the exact notice.

Comprehension rehearsal checks that a participant can identify the emergency fallback, demonstrate
voice/photo/pin reporting without entering danger, distinguish provider acceptance from dispatch,
and explain how to seek help or withdraw. Store only approved evidence references—not personal data—in
the repository.
